BODY:
As a liberal mom of three, I never thought I'd find myself so profoundly disappointed in the Democratic Party. Yet here I am, standing shoulder to shoulder with countless moms across our state - moms who are fed up, disillusioned, and angry.
Moms will always vote for the better future of their children. It's not a party issue - it's a human issue.
The Democratic Party, the supposed champion of equity and opportunity, has turned a blind eye to one of the most critical issues facing families today: the future of our children's education.
Broken education
And while the Democrats have taken commendable steps in areas like healthcare reform and climate action, they've failed miserably where it matters most to families like mine: fixing a broken education system. Their resistance to educational scholarship tax credit programs is more than just misguided policy; it's a betrayal of the very families they claim to represent.
For too long, the Democratic Party has clung to an outdated belief that public schools can meet the needs of every child. But the truth is, they can't. Public schools were built on a one-size-fits-all model that no longer works in today's world. Children are unique, with diverse talents, challenges, and dreams. They don't fit neatly into boxes-and our education system shouldn't ask them to.
The pandemic ripped the curtain off this issue. Parents watched their children struggle as schools scrambled to adapt to remote learning. We saw just how unprepared and inflexible the system really was. Moms from every political background began demanding better. We wanted schools to innovate, to be flexible, to meet our children where they are - not where some outdated system says they should be.
Instead of offering solutions, Democrats doubled down on their opposition to programs like educational scholarship tax credits. These programs allow businesses and individuals to redirect a portion of their taxes to fund scholarships for children to attend the school of their choice. For countless families, these scholarships are the only way to access the education their kids deserve.
No to families and children
But Democrats have said no. No to empowering families with choices. No to offering an escape for kids trapped in failing schools. No to providing working-class families the same opportunities that wealthy families take for granted.
Why? Why should only the wealthy be able to send their children to schools that meet their needs? Why should moms like me have to fight tooth and nail for opportunities that should be available to everyone?
This issue is personal for me. As a child, I was a recipient of a scholarship funded through an educational tax credit program. My mom, a survivor of domestic abuse, used that scholarship to send me to a school that understood my struggles and helped me thrive. That opportunity changed my life. It saved my future.
But as an adult, watching other families face the same barriers my mom did, I realized just how broken the system still is. I built a company dedicated to growing scholarship tax credit funding so that families like mine could have choices. In just a few years, RedefinED has helped thousands of children access the education they deserve.
This isn't about abandoning public schools. It's about recognizing that public schools alone can't meet the diverse needs of every child. It's about ensuring that every family - regardless of income - has access to the best possible education for their children.
Alienating Democrats
Democrats, wake up. The longer you ignore this issue, the more you alienate the very voters you claim to champion: working families, single moms, and parents who just want the best for their kids.
Educational choice isn't a partisan issue. It's a parent issue. And if Democrats don't start listening to moms, they will continue to lose elections. Moms like me are tired of being told to settle for a system that doesn't work. We're tired of hearing "no" when it comes to giving our kids the future they deserve.
Until Democrats embrace practical solutions like tax credit scholarships, they will keep losing the trust - and the votes - of parents. Because at the end of the day, moms will always choose a better future for their children.
Alyse Maslonik, a mother of three and a Pittsburgh Magazine's Women in Business Awardee, is the founder and CEO of RedefinED.
