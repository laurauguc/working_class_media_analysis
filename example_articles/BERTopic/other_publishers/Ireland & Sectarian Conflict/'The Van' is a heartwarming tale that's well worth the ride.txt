Colm Meany and Donal O'Kelly cook up some heartwarming entertainment in "The Van," the conclusion to the Irish-made Barrytown Trilogy.
 The comic-drama joins "The Commitments" and "The Snapper" in chronicling life in Barrytown, a working-class neighborhood in Dublin, Ireland.
It's a place where people act gruff but are teddy bears at heart, a neighborhood where folks sincerely care about one another.
 Best friends Meany and O'Kelly are unemployed. They decide to join forces to buy a catering van from which they plan to sell fish and chips. They have no idea what they are getting themselves into. But that doesn't matter, because even after being in business for a couple of months, they still have no idea what they're doing.
 The budgets of the movies in the trilogy have grown as the films have met with success. Director Stephen Frears has used that money to enhance the production - including bringing in Eric Clapton to provide the soundtrack - without losing the story's small, heartfelt tone. As a result, "The Van" offers a very enjoyable ride.
