The weekly magazine of former Prime Minister Bulent Ecevit was confiscated today for ''discrediting the security forces'' in an editorial in its last issue.
When he was informed about the court decision confiscating the issue, Mr. Ecevit expressed surprise and said that he did not think there was anything in it that was against the law.
Turkish court orders confiscattion of weekly magazine of former Prime Minister Bulent Ecevit
Mr. Ecevit, who was held for a month under ''protective custody'' after the army takeover of the Government in September, had promised in a written document ''not to become involved in politics and not to make political statements.'' However, he has been critical of the present military regime, and in the last issue the magazine, ''Search,'' wrote that it was the working class who would pay the bill for the economic stabilization program of the Government.
